# The 5-Color Palette

1. Primary Focus: #D80B80 (Vibrant Magenta) — The dominant hue for maximum brand recognition.
2. Secondary Accent: #FDDF2C (Bright Gold/Yellow) — A high-energy pop color for key highlights and actions.
3. Deep Contrast Dark: #2B0F54 (Midnight Plum) — A very dark, rich purple that grounds the palette and serves as a highly readable text or background shade.
4. Mid-Tone Bridge: #8E16AA (Electric Violet) — A cool, rich purple that sits perfectly between the magenta and midnight plum on the color wheel.
5. Neutral Light Background: #FAFAFC (Cool Off-White) — A crisp, clean surface shade with a microscopic hint of blue-violet to keep components feeling modern and light.
